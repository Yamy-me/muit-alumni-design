For graduates

  • Sign up with your email, then add your institution, programme and graduation year.
  • Add your workplace and upload a resume as PDF or Word.
  • Company contacts open after you apply for a job.

For companies

  • Fill in the company profile and contacts.
  • Wait for verification by the IUIT administrator.
  • Publish jobs and review applications with resumes.
